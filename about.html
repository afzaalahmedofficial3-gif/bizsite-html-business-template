Our Story

Started in a garage, grown by referrals

NovaEdge began in 2014 as two freelancers and a borrowed desk. Our first client was a local bakery that needed its first website — and twelve years later, they are still with us.

Since then we have delivered more than 250 projects for clients in retail, law, healthcare, hospitality and SaaS. We never outsourced, never white-labelled, and never chased growth for its own sake: almost every new project still arrives through a recommendation from an existing client.

  • 250+ projects launched across 14 industries
  • 92% of new work comes from client referrals
  • Average client relationship of 4+ years

Our Values

The principles behind every project
